Quarterly Engineering Review — Q2

The platform team shipped the new ingestion pipeline, cutting median event
latency from 4.2s to 0.9s. Reliability improved: there were two incidents this
quarter, both resolved within the SLA, down from five last quarter. The team
also paid down significant tech debt by retiring the legacy queue.

Hiring came in under plan: three of five open roles were filled. The biggest
risk for next quarter is the database migration, which is on the critical path
for the billing rework and has no slack in the schedule.
